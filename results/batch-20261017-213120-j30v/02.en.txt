Chapter 2: The 8 O'Clock Small Conference Room

The small conference room had only six seats. Director Zhou sat in the one closest to the door, an untouched Americano in front of her.

"Three thousand four hundred and twelve." She flipped to the last page of the spreadsheet Shen Che had sent. "What do you make of it?"

"Two possibilities," Shen Che said. "One, some version of Ye Hang had a problem during its automatic training in the early morning and misclassified a batch of users as graylist. The other is that someone is using Ye Hang's name to alter data."

"Which do you lean toward?"

"The second. When a model goes wrong, the errors are random. These three thousand–plus accounts aren't random." He pulled up a chart. "All of them reported the same merchant in the past month."

Director Zhou said nothing. She had been at Guanlan for nine years, rising from data annotation to head of risk control, and Shen Che had rarely seen her pause this long.

"What's this merchant called?"

"The backend only shows an ID number. But I looked it up—that number corresponds to an ad client we signed last quarter, called 'Qingye Life.'"

Director Zhou turned her coffee cup half a rotation.

"Shen Che," she said, "you're the head of the Review Team. The Review Team's job is to review labels, not to investigate ad clients."

"I know."

"Then you should know that this matter ends here. Those three thousand accounts—I'll have tech restore them all to the whitelist this afternoon. You write a report, and put the cause as 'model anomaly.'"

Shen Che didn't agree right away. He thought of the question Lu Nan had asked in the early hours.

"Director Zhou, if it's a model anomaly, then why did Ye Hang only change labels between one and two in the morning?"

"Because that's the automatic training window."

"Then why did it only change the people who reported Qingye Life?"

Director Zhou glanced at him. It was a brief glance, but Shen Che understood it: she had thought of it too, and she had thought of it earlier than he had.

"It's eight twenty," Director Zhou said, standing up. "Get me the report before you leave. As for Xiao Lu, tell her to stop pulling logs."

She walked to the door, then stopped.

"Shen Che, you know how your Shifu left Guanlan back then, don't you?"

Shen Che's Shifu was Gu Heng, the first head of the Review Team, who had left three years ago "for personal reasons." When Shen Che joined, Gu Heng had only mentored him for two months.

"I know," Shen Che said.

"Good." With that, Director Zhou closed the door.
